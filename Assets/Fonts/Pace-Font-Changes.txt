Pace Sans is an app-local derivative of Inter 4.1 under the SIL Open Font License. The ASCII tilde (U+007E) outline is vertically centered on the numeral zero in each weight, with glyph hinting removed. Dependent tilde variants inherit the adjustment. All advances and unrelated glyph outlines remain unchanged. Internal family names identify this modified version. Original copyright and license are in Inter-LICENSE.txt.
